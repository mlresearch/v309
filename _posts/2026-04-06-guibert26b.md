title: Why Measuring AI Environmental Impact of Organisations is Non-Trivial?
abstract: 'This paper presents the results and conclusions from the EIEIAE project,
  which defined a methodology to measure the environmental impact of AI services within
  companies and organisations. Several lessons can be learned from this project, as
  it highlighted several challenges that emerged when trying to produce reliable estimates
  of such impacts: (a) the industrial lack of transparency of AI providers, (b) the
  absence of accurate and exhaustive modelling of ICT environmental impacts, (c) the
  inherent complexity of gathering the necessary data for organisations, and (d) the
  fact that organisations are more focused on cost savings than on reducing their
  environmental impact.'
